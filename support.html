Contact

Questions, bug reports, and feature requests go through the public tracker: github.com/agezco/packetly-site/issues. Open a new issue and we will reply there — usually within a few business days. A free GitHub account is required to post.

Please include iOS version and Packetly version (Settings → tap the version line to copy it).

Packetly Pro

  • Pro is a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription, or a one-time lifetime unlock.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase.
  • Subscriptions renew unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription: iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.
  • Lifetime does not renew. Restore it on another device with Packetly → Settings → Restore Purchase.
  • Already purchased on another device: Restore Purchase in Settings.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Permissions

  • Location — optional, only to show Wi‑Fi SSID.
  • Local network — traceroute, ping, and port checks on your LAN.
  • Notifications — local SSL/domain expiry reminders only (no remote push).

Common issues

  • Ping is not ICMP. Packetly measures TCP connect latency to port 443/80.
  • Some domains have no expiry date. Not every TLD publishes RDAP.
  • Traceroute shows * for some hops. Those routers filter ICMP; this is normal.
