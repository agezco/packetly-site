Packetly Support

Privacy Policy

Effective 16 August 2026 · Packetly for iOS

Packetly is a network diagnostics app. It runs checks you start (ping, DNS, TLS, ports, traceroute, HTTP headers, and similar) and stores results on your device. This policy describes what happens to information when you use the app.

We do not run an account or analytics backend

Packetly does not create user accounts. We do not operate a Packetly cloud that stores your check history. The app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Depending on how you use the app, Packetly may store locally:

  • Favorites, recent hosts, and check history
  • SSL and domain expiry watchlists and alert thresholds
  • Language preference
  • Whether Pro features are unlocked (subscription or lifetime status mirrored from Apple)

This data stays on the device unless you export or share a report yourself (Share sheet, Markdown, or CSV).

Location

If you allow Location When In Use, Packetly may use it only to show the current Wi‑Fi network name (SSID). Location is optional. We do not use location to track you, and we do not send location to a Packetly server.

Local network

Packetly may access your local network so traceroute, ping, and port checks can reach devices on your Wi‑Fi (for example a router or NAS). Checks run only when you start them.

Network checks go to hosts you specify

When you look up a host, the app contacts that host and, depending on the tool, public infrastructure such as DNS-over-HTTPS resolvers (Cloudflare or Google, or a custom resolver you enter), RDAP/WHOIS-style registry endpoints, and Certificate Transparency (crt.sh). Those operators have their own privacy policies. Packetly does not receive a copy of those queries on a server we control.

“My IP” lookup

The dashboard shows your public IP address and its network operator. To do that, the app queries public IP-echo and IP-information services (such as ipify, icanhazip, seeip, ident.me, checkip.amazonaws.com, ipapi.co, and ipwho.is). Those services necessarily see the IP address the request comes from, and each has its own privacy policy. The result is shown on the device and is not sent to a Packetly server.

Notifications

Expiry reminders are scheduled locally on the device. Packetly does not use remote push notifications and does not send notification content to a Packetly server.

Purchases

Pro is sold through Apple as auto-renewable subscriptions (monthly and yearly) and as a one-time lifetime unlock. Apple handles payment, receipts, and entitlement status. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Children

Packetly is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Effective” date above.

Contact

Privacy questions: open an issue at github.com/agezco/packetly-site/issues, or see Support.
